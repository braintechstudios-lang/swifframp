FAQ

Questions buyers ask

What is the minimum deposit?

$100 for the Edge and Razor bots. Below a set balance they trade one position at a time with no lot multiplier, so a small account is not hit by the full-size drop. In our backtests from $100 none fell below about $85. Each card in the marketplace shows the balance where it switches to normal trading. The Shadow bots and Fast have no small-account mode: Shadow 1.49a and 1.50 also survived from $100 in our backtest, Shadow 1.49 and Fast need $200, and Shadow 1.52 needs $300. Each has an Edge version for $100 accounts. News Trading needs about $200, because it risks 5% of the balance on each trade.

Which broker should I use?

Any low-cost MT5 broker with XAUUSD. We test on Fusion Markets' raw account.

Does it work on prop firm accounts?

Yes. Load the set file for your account size. Use Shadow 1.49a, or Shadow 1.52 with news off.

Do I need a VPS?

The bot trades only while MT5 is open. A VPS keeps it on 24/5.

How does the licence work?

One key, tied to the MT5 account number you enter at checkout.

Will it win every month?

No. Expect losing days and weeks. Trade only money you can afford to lose.

Are updates free?

Yes, for the version you bought.

How do I pay?

In crypto: USDT, Bitcoin or Ethereum. No cards.

Can I pay with someone else's crypto?

No. Pay only from your own wallet or exchange account, with money that is legally yours. Fraudulent or third-party funds are refused and the order or account is cancelled.

How were the results tested?

In the MT5 strategy tester on every tick based on real ticks, 2 March to 18 November 2026, with spread and commission included.

What is Trade with us?

A pool you fund with USDT, from $100. Profit is split 70/30 each week and losses are shared. You can lose part or all of your stake.

Can I sell my own EA here?

Yes. Send a listing from the Sell page. We keep 15% of each sale.

Can I get a refund?

No. All payments are final. Check the bot and your MT5 account number before you pay.
