Pool terms
  • You sign up by connecting a Phantom wallet. All your payouts go to that wallet.
  • You send USDT on Solana to Swifframp's wallet. Swifframp holds it and trades it.
  • A confirmed deposit starts trading at the next weekly start, Monday 00:00 UTC.
  • Each week's profit is split by stake size. 70% is paid to your wallet. The rest is Swifframp's fee.
  • In a losing week every stake drops by the same percent. No fee is charged.
  • After a loss you get no profit payout until your own loss is recovered.
  • You can ask to withdraw at any time. It is paid after that week is settled.

Privacy

We collect only what each feature needs, and we never sell it.
